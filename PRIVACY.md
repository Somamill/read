# read. privacy policy

Effective 13 November 2026. Publisher: Max Miller. Support: k4jenx@gmail.com.

## App Store edition for iPhone, iPad and Mac

read. stores imported books, articles, highlights and reading progress locally. It does not include advertising, tracking SDKs or publisher-operated analytics. No account is required for local reading. Text-to-speech uses Apple's system speech services.

Optional connections send data directly to the services you choose. A Calibre/OPDS server receives library requests and credentials; a configured sync server receives reading progress and highlights. Readwise receives your authorized highlight/article requests and changes. Hardcover receives authorized book and reading-progress updates. Feed, cover and article hosts receive network requests when their content is fetched, including the network information ordinarily necessary to respond. These providers apply their own privacy policies and retention practices. You can use local files without configuring these connections.

Passwords and API tokens use the operating system's secure storage. Books and the library database use the app's private storage. Apple's device backup settings may include local app data. Removing books or highlights deletes their local records; deleting the app removes its local library. System secure-storage credentials may survive reinstall. Data already synchronized to another provider must be managed with that provider. Exported backups and copies you share remain where you save or send them.

The app uses the system file picker to access files you select. Your chosen cloud-file provider may download them. Local-network access is used only to connect to a server you configure. Microphone recording, advertising identifiers and biometric authentication are not required by the Store edition.

Contacting support sends whatever you choose to include through your email provider. Do not include passwords, tokens or private books. Support email is used to respond to your request; ask for deletion when the request is resolved.

## Full community Mac edition

The separately distributed full Mac edition can also connect to local library tools and services, import data from other applications, and run external components at your request. Its repositories and settings describe those additional integrations. These components are excluded from the mobile-based, sandboxed Store build.

## Changes and requests

Updates to this policy are published here. Contact k4jenx@gmail.com for questions or deletion requests concerning information you sent to the publisher. Local files, Apple backups and third-party accounts remain under your and their providers' controls.
