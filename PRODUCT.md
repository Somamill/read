# read.

A free, open-source reading app for books, articles and highlights. macOS is the installed desktop host; the Expo Go iPhone client connects to configured services. Calibre, KOSync, Apple Books, Hardcover and Readwise are optional connections with different capabilities. Never label an unverified connection or unsupported cross-format position conversion as working.

The main task is to continue reading the right work, with a clear available file and local highlights. Secondary tasks are saving articles, reviewing highlights, discovering books and maintaining connections. Keep operational details out of primary reading views. Support keyboard navigation, Japanese text, user-selected reader fonts and accessibility preferences. Preserve files, reading history and credentials during visual updates.
